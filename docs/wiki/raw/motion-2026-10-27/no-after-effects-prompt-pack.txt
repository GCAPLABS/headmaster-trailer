The prompt                                    COPY-READY          390 WORDS




Paste it as is into Claude Code running Opus 5.5 at effort high, with Blender 5, Node with puppeteer and ffmpeg installed.
It writes the interface as HTML, the scene as Python, renders headless on the GPU and checks its own stills.



   BRAND
   HERO: Clawd, the Claude Code mascot as one smooth toy: body 12x8x6 units, two side nubs, four short legs, two black eye
   slits, soft rounded bevels, no voxel grid, terracotta D97757
   APP: a pixel-faithful After Effects-style workspace, dark theme: menu bar, toolbar (Home, Selection ... Rectangle, Pen, Type
   ... Puppet), Project, Composition, Info, Preview, Effects & Presets searched for "glow", and a Timeline with five layers and
   open Position and Scale rows. Real layout and labels, no logos
   RESULT: the comp Clawd builds: "MADE WITH CLAUDE", an orange spark, a pen swoosh, a glow, the subtitle OPUS 5.5
   SET: every panel is a floating slab over a dark warm studio; the UI glows like a screen

   FORMAT: Blender 5 Python, Cycles GPU, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 12.0 s at 120 BPM. Build the interface in HTML and screenshot it
   with puppeteer into a 3840x2160 texture; draw the comp and the timecode on a canvas, one PNG per frame. One timeline.py holds
   the layout in UI pixels and every event; the HTML, the Blender scene and the sound all read it.

   BEATS (seconds):
   0-1.5 Clawd is a flat clawd.png layer in the comp, selection handles around him; he knocks twice from inside
   1.5-2.0 peels off the screen as a flat card and pops into 3D; the viewer glass cracks, shards fly
   2.5-3.5 lands on Type, Rectangle and Pen: each press types the title, pops the spark, draws the swoosh
   4.5-5.5 flips across to Effects > Glow, a Glow chip pops up, he kicks it into the comp: glow on
   6.0-7.5 four hops along the Position row; each stamps a keyframe and the comp jumps to that pose
   8.0-9.6 hops on the playhead, it rewinds to 0, he surfs it to the end while the comp plays
   10-12 Preview > Play; every other panel powers down and falls away; Clawd waves next to the finished comp

   CAMERA: starts wide on the whole workspace, follows every hop at Clawd height with shallow focus, whips to each button, ends
   on the comp alone.

   LOOK: UI as emission under black glossy glass; keep the lights out of the UI reflections so nothing blows out; bloom, 0.5
   shutter blur, AgX; the body must read D97757.

   CHECKS: render the UI texture and a comp contact sheet first, then 18 stills at half res before the full render.




BRAND                    The block you swap. HERO, APP, RESULT, SET. The rest of the prompt only uses these names.


APP                      Name every panel and the state it is in. The more real the labels, the more the film reads as the real app.


FORMAT                   HTML for the interface, a canvas for the comp, Blender for the world: each tool does what it is best at.


BEATS                    Every hop lands on something that changes the comp. A jump that changes nothing is cut.


LOOK                     The one line that fixed the first previews: glossy screens and big area lights fight.


CHECKS                   Makes Opus look before it spends GPU time. Most of the polish comes from this line.

Render it fast
1. Build the UI: python build_ui.py, then node render_ui.js for ui.png, the 360 comp frames and the timecode.
2. Build the .blend from scene.py with blender -b --factory-startup -P scene.py.

Sound                      COPY-READY       220 WORDS




The picture comes from the prompt on page 2; the sound came from this one more prompt, driven by the same
timeline.py.


   Add sound. Synthesize everything in Python with numpy, scipy and pedalboard: no samples, no words. 48 kHz stereo WAV, 12.0 s,
   read every event from timeline.py and land each hit on its frame.

   Music: a cute lo-fi beat at 120 BPM. Before the pop only a soft pad and ticking hats; the beat drops on the pop: kick, snare,
   hats, a rubbery bass on F G Am C, glassy FM keys on the off-beats, a kick pump on the bed. On Play a C major brass stab, then
   the chord rings out to a 0.5 s fade.

   Effects: two glass knocks, a sticker peel, a pop with breaking glass and a sub boom; a boing on every take-off and a soft
   pomf on every landing, whooshes on the big leaps; a chunky keycap click and a UI blip on each tool, a key tap for every typed
   letter, a bloop for the spark, a pen scribble; a thwack for the kick, a whoosh for the chip and a magic shimmer with bells
   for the glow; rising glockenspiel dings C E G C for the keyframes; tape-rewind chirps, a skate roll and scrub ticks for the
   playhead; a power-down zap and a whoosh for each falling panel; yay chirps and sparkles at the end.

   Master: a compressor and a limiter at -1 dBFS.




The check I ran
LOUDNESS PER HALF SECOND, BEFORE ANY SOUND FOLLOW-UP

-28 dB in the first half second, -21 dB during the knocks, -14 dB on the peel, then -11 to -12 dB from the pop to the end, and -19
dB in the fade. The drop lands where the picture explodes and nothing pokes out above the groove, so no sound follow-up was needed.


Into the MP4: the WAV was muxed in as the video's AAC 320k track with ffmpeg; the same step works for your own video.
