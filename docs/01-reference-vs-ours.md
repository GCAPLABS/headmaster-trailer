# Launch trailer: reference vs ours

Nov 6, 2026 · @Matvey Zarif

Only their first 15 seconds is a fast-cut montage. A calm narrator carries the other 89 seconds, and each shot holds for 2 to 8 seconds while the voice explains it. Our new opening now matches their first 21 seconds. What we still lack:

- a narrator over the whole film
- a middle act where the to-do list "evolved" feature by feature
- a slow, specific problem act
- the logo forming out of "Until now"
- real product screens
- a 3D gallery
- a white end card

Theirs runs 104 seconds. Ours is planned at 64, which is too short to hold a narrator, so the plan below moves to about 95 seconds.

## Gaps, biggest first

1. **No narrator.** Their energy after 0:15 comes from the voice, not from cuts. Ours has music only, so the story never lands.
2. **No change of pace.** They go fast for 15 seconds, then slow right down. Each idea gets one clear, long shot. v1 stayed at one speed the whole way.
3. **No "it evolved" act.** They show five real features, one shot each. Our list needs the same: paper, a pocket organiser, reminders, sync, sharing, nagging badges.
4. **The problem is concrete for them.** A real email with the asks highlighted, then an empty reply waiting. Ours needs a real message like "Can you book the dentist and send the form by Friday?" with each task highlighted, then an empty list waiting.
5. **The logo is built from the words.** The "o" in "Until now" becomes the mark. Ours should build the Headmaster mark from the word itself.
6. **Real product screens.** They show their actual app. We should use real Headmaster screens: WhatsApp, the agent at work, and the approval step.
7. **One look for "ready".** A soft aura with one card per result, then two short lines on their own. We need our own version in Studio lavender, not their colours.
8. **The human presses Send.** That is the end of the product act. Ours is the Approve tap.
9. **The gallery is 3D.** We can fake depth with perspective in Remotion: plinths, a slow dolly, and our icon on the last plinth.
10. **White end card.** "Get early access" (or "Free in beta"), the icon, headmaster.app.
11. **Length.** About 95 seconds instead of 64. Music take A ends too early, so we need a longer cut or a second track.
